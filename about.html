ABOUT CYBER SAATHI

Cybersecurity
shouldn't feel complicated.

Cyber Saathi is a student-built cybersecurity education platform designed to make digital safety easier to understand and easier to practice.

THE IDEA

Why Cyber Saathi?

Students spend a large part of their lives online. We study online, communicate online, create accounts, share information, and increasingly depend on digital services.

Yet cybersecurity can often feel like something complicated, technical, or meant only for experts.

Cyber Saathi was created to change that. It turns important cybersecurity concepts into simple lessons, practical examples, and interactive challenges that students can actually use.

THE CREATOR

Built by a student,
for students.

Cyber Saathi began as a personal project to explore cybersecurity, web development, and the ways technology can be used to help people protect themselves online.

Instead of building another project that simply demonstrates code, the goal was to create something useful—something a student could actually visit, learn from, and use.
