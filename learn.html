THREAT 01

Phishing

Phishing is a type of cyberattack where someone pretends to be a trustworthy person or organization to trick you into giving away information.

How does it work?

An attacker might send an email, text message, or social media message that looks legitimate. They may ask you to click a link, open an attachment, or provide sensitive information.

⚠️ Saathi Tip

Don't click a link just because a message looks official. Check the sender, the URL, and the reason you're being asked to act urgently.

THREAT 02

Password Security

A strong password is one of the simplest ways to protect your online accounts.

What makes a password strong?

Strong passwords are long, unique, and difficult for other people to guess. Avoid using easily available information such as your name, birthday, school, or phone number.

🔐 Saathi Tip

Never reuse the same password for important accounts. Consider using a password manager to create and store unique passwords.

03

THREAT 03

Social Engineering

Social engineering attacks manipulate people rather than directly attacking a computer system.

Why does it work?

Attackers may create fear, urgency, curiosity, or trust to influence someone into making a decision they normally wouldn't make.

🧠 Saathi Tip

If someone pressures you to act immediately, stop and verify the request through another trusted method.

04

THREAT 04

Malware

Malware is malicious software designed to damage systems, steal information, disrupt activities, or gain unauthorized access.

Common types

Viruses, worms, trojans, ransomware, and spyware are different forms of malware. They can spread through malicious downloads, attachments, websites, or compromised software.

🛡️ Saathi Tip

Keep your operating system and software updated, and avoid downloading programs from untrusted sources.

05

THREAT 05

Online Privacy

Your online activity can reveal more information about you than you might realize.

What should you protect?

Personal information such as your address, phone number, passwords, location, school details, and private photographs should be shared carefully.

🔒 Saathi Tip

Review the privacy settings of your social media accounts and think before sharing personal information publicly.

06

THREAT 06

Safe Browsing

Safe browsing means developing habits that reduce the risks you encounter while using the internet.

Simple habits matter

Be careful with unfamiliar websites, downloads, pop-ups, and links. Check website addresses before entering sensitive information.

🌐 Saathi Tip

If a website or message feels suspicious, don't interact with it. When in doubt, leave the page and verify the information.
